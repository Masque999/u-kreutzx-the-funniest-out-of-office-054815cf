# The Funniest Out-of-Office

One original line of an out-of-office auto-reply, at most 140 characters, as funny as possible.

Entry: `I am out of the office and also out of patience, so your email has joined a queue that is currently longer than my vacation.`

Run: `python3 main.py` (writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`).
